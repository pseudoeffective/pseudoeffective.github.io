## Math

Here is a proof-of-concept for turning a paper into an interactive web document.  The design is based on David Muñoz-Lahoz's gorgeous [Bourgain digestion](https://bourgain-talagrand.munozlahoz.xyz).  I asked Claude-Opus-5.5 to produce a tool for converting a LaTeX article into a web doc of this type; it took 15-20 minutes of back-and-forth to get things looking right.
